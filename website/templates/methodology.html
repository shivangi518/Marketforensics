Methodology

Transparent by design

Every threshold and data boundary is explicit.

01

Collect

Download adjusted daily OHLCV data for NSE stocks and Nifty.

02

Align

Use the Nifty calendar. Remove invalid closes. Do not forward-fill.

03

Detect

Flag moves at least 2.5× the prior 20-day volatility.

04

Describe

Build return, volatility, volume, momentum, and Nifty-relative features.

05

Label

Apply market-wide, stock-specific, volume-shock, then unknown rules.

06

Evaluate

Train on older dates and score on later dates.
